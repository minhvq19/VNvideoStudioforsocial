Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how VideoStudio ("we", "our", or "the Application") handles data. VideoStudio is a private, internal desktop application built exclusively for our organization's media management and is not available for public commercial use or public registration.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Because VideoStudio is an internal workflow tool, we do not collect, process, track, or sell any personal data from the general public. The only data processed by the Application pertains exclusively to our own authorized team members and our own official social media accounts.

  • OAuth Authentication Data: We use authorized API endpoints (such as TikTok Login Kit) to authenticate our internal accounts. The application only reads basic profile information (e.g., user.info.basic) necessary to confirm a successful connection and display the active profile within our local dashboard.
  • Media Content: The application processes video and image files locally on the user's machine before transmitting them securely to our linked social media channels via authorized APIs.

2. Third-Party API Compliance

VideoStudio integrates with third-party platforms strictly via their official Developer APIs. We maintain absolute compliance with their respective Developer Terms, API Guidelines, and Data Privacy policies.

  • We do not scrape, crawl, or extract any user data, analytics, or background information from third-party platforms.
  • Access tokens generated via OAuth are encrypted and stored locally. They are used exclusively for publishing content to our own channels and are never shared, transmitted, or sold to external servers or third parties.

3. Data Security and Retention

Security is a fundamental component of our internal architecture. All communication between the VideoStudio desktop client and third-party APIs utilizes secure, encrypted protocols (HTTPS/TLS). Authentication tokens are automatically revoked or refreshed in accordance with platform policies, and we do not retain API tokens beyond their necessary operational lifespan.

4. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our internal operations or API provider requirements. Since this is an internal tool, updates will be communicated directly to authorized personnel.
