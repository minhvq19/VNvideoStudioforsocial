Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how VideoStudio ("we", "our", or "the Application") handles data. VideoStudio is a private, internal desktop application built exclusively for our organization's media management. It is not available for public commercial use.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Because VideoStudio is an internal workflow tool, we do not collect, process, track, or sell any personal data from the general public. The application uses authorized OAuth API endpoints to connect our internal accounts securely. The application only reads basic profile information necessary to confirm a successful connection.

2. Third-Party Platform Compliance

VideoStudio integrates with third-party platforms (TikTok, Meta, Google) strictly via their official Developer APIs. We maintain absolute compliance with their Developer Terms and Data Privacy policies.

  • YouTube API Services: Our application uses YouTube API Services. By using this integration, authorized internal users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • We do not scrape, crawl, or extract any user data from these platforms.
  • Access tokens generated via OAuth are encrypted and stored locally. They are used exclusively for publishing content to our own channels and are never shared.

3. Data Security and Revocation

Security is a fundamental component of our internal architecture. Authentication tokens are not retained beyond their operational lifespan. Furthermore, users can revoke the application's access to their data via the respective third-party security settings pages (e.g., Google Security Settings, TikTok App Permissions).
