VideoStudio

Internal Media Management Workspace

VideoStudio is a proprietary desktop client developed exclusively for our internal operations. It provides our media team with a secure, centralized dashboard to manage digital assets, coordinate publishing schedules, and streamline the distribution of approved media to our official channels.

Core Capabilities:
  • Encrypted OAuth 2.0 integration for secure platform access.
  • Centralized staging environment for approved media files.
  • Internal workflow and scheduling alignment.
