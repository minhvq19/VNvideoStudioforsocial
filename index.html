Multi-Platform API Automation Workspace

VideoStudio is an internal desktop application providing our media team with a secure, centralized dashboard to manage and distribute approved media to our official channels across TikTok, Meta, and YouTube.

Strict Adherence to TikTok Policies

Our integration strictly enforces TikTok's Developer Terms and Community Guidelines. The system operates solely on the video.upload and video.publish scopes for our own authenticated channels.

  • Content Integrity: Zero tolerance for hate speech, misinformation, or sexually explicit material. All media undergoes internal human review prior to API submission.
  • Authentic Behavior: VideoStudio is designed strictly for legitimate publishing. We forbid API spamming, artificial metric inflation, or coordinated inauthentic behavior.
  • IP Protection: We restrict uploads to original content owned by our organization, actively preventing copyright infringement and unauthorized watermarks.

Meta Platform & Graph API Compliance

VideoStudio integrates with the Instagram Graph API and Facebook Login for Business to manage our professional creator/business accounts securely.

  • Authorized Access: We utilize official OAuth 2.0 protocols to link our internal Facebook Pages and Professional Instagram accounts without compromising credentials.
  • Content Standards: All assets pushed via the Graph API comply strictly with Meta's Community Standards and Platform Terms.
  • Data Minimization: We only request scopes necessary for content publishing (e.g., instagram_content_publish) and do not crawl or extract public user data from the Meta ecosystem.

YouTube API Services Guidelines

Our application utilizes YouTube API Services to automate video uploads to our proprietary YouTube channels. By using this integration, we inherently comply with Google's ecosystem rules.

  • Terms of Service: Our internal users are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Privacy Policy: We adhere to the Google Privacy Policy. We do not collect or misuse user data via the YouTube Data API.
  • Anti-Spam: VideoStudio prohibits the manipulation of video metadata, tags, or thumbnails to deceive users, fully respecting YouTube's spam and deceptive practices policies.
