Internal Media Workflow Automation

VideoStudio is a proprietary desktop client developed exclusively for internal operations. It provides our media team with a secure, centralized workspace to manage digital assets, coordinate publishing schedules, and streamline the distribution of approved media to our official channels via authorized API integrations.

Strict Adherence to TikTok Platform Policies

VideoStudio is engineered with compliance at its core. Our internal workflows strictly enforce TikTok's developer and community standards to ensure absolute platform integrity. We mandate operational compliance with:

  • Community Guidelines: Zero tolerance for content involving violence, hate speech, misinformation, or sexually explicit material. All internal media undergoes strict human review prior to any API submission.
  • Intellectual Property Policy: VideoStudio restricts uploads exclusively to original content owned by our organization. We strictly prohibit copyright infringement, unauthorized watermarks, and counterfeit material.
  • Integrity and Authenticity: Our system is designed solely for legitimate internal publishing. We strictly forbid the use of our integration for API spamming, artificial metric inflation (fake likes/views), or coordinated inauthentic behavior.
  • Data Privacy API Terms: OAuth tokens are securely encrypted on the local machine. We do not crawl, scrape, or extract public user data from TikTok. The integration is strictly limited to the video.upload and video.publish endpoints for our own authenticated accounts.
